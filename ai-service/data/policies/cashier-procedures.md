# Billix Demo Cashier Procedures

## Starting a shift

The cashier must sign in with their own account, verify the assigned branch, and confirm the opening cash amount before processing transactions.

## Completing a sale

The cashier should scan each product, confirm quantity and price, select the customer's payment method, and provide a receipt after payment succeeds.

## Voiding an item

An item may be removed before payment when the customer changes their mind or the item was scanned incorrectly. The void reason should be recorded.

## Cancelling a completed transaction

A completed transaction must not be deleted. Corrections must follow the refund process and may require manager approval.

## Account security

Cashiers must not share accounts or leave an authenticated register unattended. Suspicious activity should be reported to the branch manager.
