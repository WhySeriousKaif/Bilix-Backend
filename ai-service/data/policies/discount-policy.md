# Billix Demo Discount Policy

## Cashier discounts

Cashiers may apply a discount of up to five percent when a valid active promotion permits it. The reason for the discount must be recorded in the transaction.

## Manager approval

Discounts above five percent require manager approval. A discount may never reduce the selling price below the configured minimum price.

## Combining promotions

Two promotions cannot be combined unless the promotion rules explicitly allow stacking. The system-calculated promotion should be used when there is a conflict.

## Damaged packaging

A manager may approve a discount for damaged packaging when the product itself is safe and usable. The adjustment reason must be recorded.
