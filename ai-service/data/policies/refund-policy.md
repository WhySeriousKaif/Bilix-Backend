# Billix Demo Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Products may be returned when they are defective, damaged at the time of sale, or different from the item shown on the receipt. A valid Billix receipt or transaction reference is required.

## Return window

Eligible products should normally be returned within seven calendar days of purchase. A store manager may review exceptional cases after this period.

## Opened products

Opened food, personal-care, and hygiene products are not normally eligible for return unless they are defective or damaged. The manager must approve any exception.

## Refund method

Approved refunds should use the original payment method whenever possible. Cash refunds for card or digital payments require manager approval.

## Final-sale products

Products clearly marked as final sale cannot be returned for change of mind. Statutory rights for defective products are not affected.
